`Pagamento` records individual payment transactions made against a service order. Each `Pagamento` represents a discrete monetary event — a full settlement, a deposit, or one instalment in a multi-payment arrangement. A single `OrdemServico` can accumulate multiple `Pagamento` records over time, enabling partial and staged payments without any special configuration.

## Fields

<ResponseField name="id" type="integer">
  Auto-generated primary key. Assigned by the database on creation.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="valor" type="float" required>
  The amount of this payment, in Brazilian Reais (BRL). Must be **greater than 0** — zero-value payments are not permitted.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="forma_pagamento" type="FormaPagamento enum">
  The payment method used. Defaults to `pix`. Accepted values:

  | Value      | Meaning                              |
  |------------|--------------------------------------|
  | `pix`      | Instant Brazilian electronic payment |
  | `credito`  | Credit card                          |
  | `debito`   | Debit card                           |
  | `dinheiro` | Cash                                 |
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="data" type="datetime">
  The timestamp of when the payment was made, in UTC. Indexed for efficient querying by date range. Defaults to the server's current time at the moment the record is created.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="ordem_servico_id" type="integer" required>
  Foreign key referencing the `OrdemServico` this payment is applied to.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="created_at" type="datetime">
  ISO 8601 timestamp of when the record was created. Set automatically by the server in UTC.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="updated_at" type="datetime">
  ISO 8601 timestamp of the last update. Refreshed automatically on every write operation in UTC.
</ResponseField>

## Payment Methods

**`pix`** — Brazil's instant payment system, operated by the Banco Central do Brasil. Transfers are settled in real time, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. This is the default `forma_pagamento`.

**`credito`** — Credit card payment. The workshop receives the amount subject to the card network's standard processing terms.

**`debito`** — Debit card payment. Funds are debited directly from the client's bank account at the point of transaction.

**`dinheiro`** — Cash payment. No electronic processing is involved.

## Business Rules

### Approved items required

Payments can only be recorded against a service order when at least one approved line item exists — that is, when one or more `ItemServico` or `ItemPeca` records on the order have `aprovado = true`. An order with no approved items has a billable total of zero, and no payment can be applied to it.

### Multiple payments per order

There is no single-payment constraint. A service order can receive any number of `Pagamento` records over its lifetime. This supports common workshop scenarios such as:

- A **deposit** taken when the client authorises the budget, followed by a **final payment** on collection.
- **Instalment payments** split across different payment methods or dates.
- A **partial payment** recorded immediately, with the remainder logged when the client returns.

The API does not enforce that the sum of all payments equals `OrdemServico.valor_total` — over- and under-payment states are both valid and must be managed at the application or reporting layer.

## Example

The following record is the full settlement for seed order #1 (routine oil change), paid in full via PIX on the day the vehicle was collected:

```json
{
  "id": 1,
  "valor": 313.00,
  "forma_pagamento": "pix",
  "data": "2025-01-01T14:30:00Z",
  "ordem_servico_id": 1,
  "created_at": "2025-01-01T14:30:00Z",
  "updated_at": "2025-01-01T14:30:00Z"
}
```

## Multiple Payments Example

The following two records illustrate how partial or staged payments appear in the system. Orders #9 and #10 — both currently `em_andamento` (in progress) — received advance payments while the work was still being carried out:

```json
[
  {
    "id": 9,
    "valor": 500.00,
    "forma_pagamento": "pix",
    "ordem_servico_id": 9
  },
  {
    "id": 10,
    "valor": 600.00,
    "forma_pagamento": "credito",
    "ordem_servico_id": 10
  }
]
```

Order #9 has a `valor_total` of R$ 1,110.00. The R$ 500.00 PIX payment represents a partial advance deposit; the remaining R$ 610.00 would be settled with an additional `Pagamento` record once the service is completed. Order #10, with a total of R$ 1,160.00, similarly shows a R$ 600.00 credit card deposit against its outstanding balance.
