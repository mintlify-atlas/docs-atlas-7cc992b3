`Pagamento` records individual payment transactions made against a service order. Each `Pagamento` represents a discrete monetary event — a full settlement, an advance deposit, or one instalment in a multi-payment arrangement. A single `OrdemServico` can accumulate multiple `Pagamento` records over its lifetime, enabling partial and staged payments without any special configuration.

This resource has no standalone route file. Payment operations are performed through the `/ordens-servico` endpoint family.

## Schema

### Request fields (create / update)

<ParamField body="valor" type="float" required>
  The amount of this payment, in Brazilian Reais (BRL). Must be **greater than 0** — zero-value payments are not permitted.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="forma_pagamento" type="FormaPagamento">
  Payment method used. Defaults to `pix`. Accepted values:

  | Value      | Meaning                              |
  |------------|--------------------------------------|
  | `pix`      | Instant Brazilian electronic payment |
  | `credito`  | Credit card                          |
  | `debito`   | Debit card                           |
  | `dinheiro` | Cash                                 |
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="data" type="datetime">
  Timestamp of when the payment was made, in UTC. Indexed for efficient date-range queries. Defaults to the server's current UTC time at record creation.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="ordem_servico_id" type="integer" required>
  Foreign key referencing the `OrdemServico` this payment is applied to.
</ParamField>

### Response fields

<ResponseField name="id" type="integer">
  Auto-generated primary key. Assigned by the database on creation.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="valor" type="float">
  Payment amount in BRL.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="forma_pagamento" type="string">
  Payment method. One of `pix`, `credito`, `debito`, or `dinheiro`.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="data" type="datetime">
  ISO 8601 UTC timestamp of when the payment was made.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="ordem_servico_id" type="integer">
  Foreign key referencing the parent `OrdemServico`.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="created_at" type="datetime">
  ISO 8601 UTC timestamp of when the record was created.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="updated_at" type="datetime">
  ISO 8601 UTC timestamp of the last update.
</ResponseField>

### Update fields (all optional)

A `PATCH` request may include any subset of the following fields.

<ParamField body="valor" type="float">
  Updated payment amount. Must be greater than 0.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="forma_pagamento" type="FormaPagamento">
  Updated payment method. One of `pix`, `credito`, `debito`, `dinheiro`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="data" type="datetime">
  Updated payment timestamp.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="ordem_servico_id" type="integer">
  Reassigns the payment to a different service order.
</ParamField>

## Error Codes

| Status | Exception                          | When it occurs                                                                   |
|--------|------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 404    | `RecursoNaoEncontradoException`    | The payment ID or the referenced `ordem_servico_id` does not exist.              |
| 409    | `ViolacaoChaveEstrangeiraException` | The supplied `ordem_servico_id` references a non-existent service order.         |
| 422    | `RegraDeNegocioException`          | The service order has no approved line items — no billable total to pay against. |

## Payment Methods

**`pix`** — Brazil's instant payment system, operated by the Banco Central do Brasil. Transfers settle in real time, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. This is the default `forma_pagamento`.

**`credito`** — Credit card payment. The workshop receives the amount subject to the card network's standard processing terms.

**`debito`** — Debit card payment. Funds are debited directly from the client's bank account at the point of transaction.

**`dinheiro`** — Cash payment. No electronic processing is involved.

## Business Rules

### Approved items required

Payments can only be recorded against a service order that has at least one approved line item — that is, at least one `ItemServico` or `ItemPeca` record on the order with `aprovado = true`. An order with no approved items has a billable total of zero and cannot receive a payment.

### Multiple payments per order

There is no single-payment constraint on `Pagamento`. A service order can receive any number of payment records over its lifetime. This supports common workshop scenarios such as:

- A **deposit** taken when the client authorises the budget, followed by a **final settlement** at vehicle collection.
- **Instalments** split across different payment methods or collection dates.
- A **partial payment** recorded immediately, with the remainder logged when the client returns.

<Note>
  The API does not enforce that the sum of all payments equals `OrdemServico.valor_total`. Over- and under-payment states are both valid; balance tracking must be handled at the application or reporting layer.
</Note>

## Examples

### Create request

The following body records a full PIX settlement of R$ 313.00 for service order 1 (routine oil change):

```json
{
  "valor": 313.00,
  "forma_pagamento": "pix",
  "data": "2025-01-01T14:30:00Z",
  "ordem_servico_id": 1
}
```

### Create response

```json
{
  "id": 1,
  "valor": 313.00,
  "forma_pagamento": "pix",
  "data": "2025-01-01T14:30:00Z",
  "ordem_servico_id": 1,
  "created_at": "2025-01-01T14:30:00Z",
  "updated_at": "2025-01-01T14:30:00Z"
}
```

### Advance deposit examples

Orders #9 and #10 are currently `em_andamento` (in progress) and each received a partial advance deposit while work was still being carried out:

```json
[
  {
    "id": 9,
    "valor": 500.00,
    "forma_pagamento": "pix",
    "data": "2025-01-14T10:00:00Z",
    "ordem_servico_id": 9,
    "created_at": "2025-01-14T10:00:00Z",
    "updated_at": "2025-01-14T10:00:00Z"
  },
  {
    "id": 10,
    "valor": 600.00,
    "forma_pagamento": "credito",
    "data": "2025-01-15T09:00:00Z",
    "ordem_servico_id": 10,
    "created_at": "2025-01-15T09:00:00Z",
    "updated_at": "2025-01-15T09:00:00Z"
  }
]
```

Order #9 has a `valor_total` of R$ 1,110.00; the R$ 500.00 PIX deposit leaves R$ 610.00 outstanding. Order #10, with a total of R$ 1,160.00, shows a R$ 600.00 credit card deposit against its outstanding balance.
